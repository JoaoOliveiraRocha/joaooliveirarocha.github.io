~90s. The ouroboros is the joke: the snake eating its own tail. Review agents are useful as an extra pass, not as the final gate. On the Markdown line: AGENTS.md, SKILL.md and prompt templates are all requests to a probabilistic model. Useful, but a request is not a guarantee; things that must never happen belong in something that can refuse (tests, linters, CI, hooks). Image: Ouroboros.svg (CC0, Wikimedia Commons).
