What to take from this

  • 🔒 Harnesses are closed — little observability, Not very flexible, little say in how they work.
  • 🪶 Pi / Oh My Pi! is minimal on purpose — Enhance it as you wish to work.
  • 🧠 Harnesses don't dramatically increase "success rate" Costs, Speed, Ease of use, Follow-ups, custom instructions, workflows tools (skills, prompts), etc are still important.
  • 🧑‍💻 Engineering is still ours — scope it, understand it, verify it, minimize its decisions. Optimize workflow. Make it behave as you intend. Reduce hallucionations.
  • 🎛️ Well defined tasks > Skills, Harness, Tools Provide full context + let it ask you. Minimize the decisions a LLM has to take. a good guideline.
